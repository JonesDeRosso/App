2. Overview and data minimisation

The app provides places, events, an offline map and local favourites. No account is required. The current version contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and no proprietary cloud synchronisation service. It does not send favourites, language preferences or search queries to a JONES DE ROSSO server.

Bundled content and maps work offline. Fetching event data nevertheless connects to the respective providers. An offline map does not mean that every app function works without external data transfers.

Allgäu Pro is unlocked through an optional one-time purchase directly from your app store. Purchases are verified through Google Play or Apple StoreKit; no additional purchase management service is used.

3. Local storage and device access

We store your chosen language, completion of onboarding and display of the startup introduction, identifiers of favourite places and events, cached events, refresh timestamps and source refresh status on your device. Search and map filters are processed in the running app state. Storage uses the Android or iOS app storage area and does not create an online account.

The purposes are to provide your preferences and favourites and retain previously loaded events during connection problems. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR insofar as processing is necessary for the app functions you request. Storage and access to technically necessary settings and caches are based on section 25(2)(2) TDDDG.

Tap a heart again to remove a favourite. Device settings let you delete app data; on iOS delete the app rather than offloading it. Restoring a device backup may restore previously saved data.

Use of the one free event update and its 30-day window is also stored locally. This limit applies to the app data on the device and is not linked to a user account. On Android, a store-signed purchase receipt is cached locally and verified on startup to support offline Pro access. Apple StoreKit manages verified purchase entitlements on the device. A simple local Pro flag is not accepted as proof of purchase.

4. Location function

When you press the location button on the map, the app checks location permission and asks the operating system for permission if necessary. It then requests your current location once. Depending on permission and device capabilities, it may be precise or approximate. Coordinates are used on your device to centre the offline map and temporarily show your position. The marker is removed after approximately five seconds.

The app does not transmit these coordinates to our server, external event providers, store a location history or track you in the background. Location determination by Android/iOS and their system services is also governed by your device or operating system provider’s settings and privacy policies.

The legal basis for optional location processing is consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw or limit permission in system settings at any time. The app remains usable without permission; centring on your location becomes unavailable. Opening the map alone does not request your location.

5. Event updates and network data

“Refresh events” fetches public calendar and event pages directly from your device. In Pro, an incoming event link may also trigger an update if the linked event is not available locally. The free update is only started using the update button. Source detail pages and technical interfaces may be requested too. Providers and their hosting/network services receive your public IP address, requested URL and technical connection data. This may include the request time, status and HTTP headers such as language and app/client identification. Sources may keep server logs.

The app does not send local favourites, precise location coordinates or personal search profiles with these requests. Events and source status are cached locally. Redirects and source interfaces may involve additional technical recipients for the same request.

The legal basis for requested updates is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. External operators’ own server logging is covered by their privacy notices. Without internet or updates, you can use bundled and previously cached content.

An update you start continues when switching between app sections. The app does not send notifications or request notification permission. On Android, the request runs in the app process; the operating system may pause or stop it when the app is in the background. On iOS, the app requests the additional background execution time permitted by the operating system. Closing the app can interrupt an ongoing request. This does not transmit additional personal data to event providers or schedule automatic recurring updates.

6. Our photo-spot images

The photo-spot photographs are owned by JONES DE ROSSO. They are bundled with the app as compressed WebP files and loaded locally. Displaying these photos does not connect to an image platform or transmit your IP address to an image provider. The bundled files contain no retained EXIF, GPS or camera metadata. The app does not read or upload photos from your personal device photo library.

7. Offline map

Raster maps, region boundaries and place coordinates are bundled with the app and processed locally. Displaying, zooming and filtering this map does not request online map tiles or send map views or positions to OpenStreetMap. OpenStreetMap is the data source, not a runtime map server for this function. External links to map or other websites are separate.

8. External links and sharing

Tapping a website link opens an external app or browser. From that point, the target provider and application’s privacy notices apply. They may process your IP address, browser information, cookies and data you enter. The app does not control their processing.

Sharing passes the selected place or event link and associated text to the system share sheet. You choose the recipient and application. That application processes the shared content under its own privacy rules. An event link may contain its identifier, date and public event URL. Your current location coordinates or entire favourites list are not automatically shared. Providing requested links and sharing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

9. Support requests

If you contact us by email, phone or our website, we process the contact details, request content and technical information you voluntarily provide for troubleshooting. The app does not automatically send support reports. Please provide only information needed for your request.

The purpose is to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual enquiries, otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our interest in answering requests. Data is retained only as long as needed to handle the request, establish or defend claims where relevant, or comply with statutory retention requirements. The website and email service are separate from the app.

10. App stores, purchases and device backups

Downloads, store accounts and billing are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. Allgäu Pro is a permanent unlock through a one-time purchase without a subscription. The app receives no complete credit card, banking or other payment details.

Purchases, restoration and verification use Google and Apple store interfaces directly. They process product identifiers, transaction IDs, receipts, purchase dates and entitlement status, plus technical device, app and network data required for the connection. The store links the purchase to your store account. The app creates no user account and sends no favourites, search queries or location coordinates to the store. No additional purchase management service is included.

Entitlements may be checked at app launch, when returning to the app and during use. Android receipts are verified locally using the store signature and cached in app storage; a successful store query without valid ownership removes cached access. On iOS, only Apple StoreKit verified, non-revoked purchases are accepted. Previously verified access may continue offline; refunds can only be reliably reflected after reconnecting.

Processing performs the purchase, securely provides Pro and restores purchases. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Required local receipts provide the requested unlock (section 25(2)(2) TDDDG). Deleting local data does not cancel a store transaction or delete store billing records. A one-time purchase can be restored with the same store account.

Android or iOS may include app data in device backups according to your settings. The backup provider's terms apply. We operate no backup server and cannot access your store account or device backup.

11. Recipients and processing outside the EEA

We do not sell local app data to advertisers or share it to build profiles. Recipients of technical network data are the event providers you access and their infrastructure operators. Opening links or sharing adds the websites, applications and recipients you select.

Device/store providers and some external event providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the USA. External sources and redirects may involve other countries. Locations, retention periods and any international transfer safeguards are described in those independently responsible providers’ privacy notices. We do not claim universal EU storage or a standard contractual clauses agreement covering every provider. Ask the respective provider or our privacy contact for details.

12. Retention and deletion

Language, onboarding status and favourites remain locally stored until changed, removed or app data is deleted. Event caches are replaced or cleaned during updates; cached data for failed sources may remain as fallback. A local cache file can remain after an event until the next cleanup or deletion of app data. Refresh information remains until overwritten or deleted.

The app does not write your current location to this persistent storage. Filters and search state reside in memory during use. External providers set their own server log retention periods. Support enquiries are retained for the purposes and statutory requirements described in section 9.

The local free-update record and Android cached signed receipt remain until app data is deleted or an invalid receipt is removed. Store transaction records and iOS entitlements are managed by the relevant store under its terms and retention obligations.

13. Your rights

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have rights of access (Article 15 GDPR), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction (Article 18) and portability (Article 20). Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time for the future without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Withdraw location permission in device settings.

For processing based on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object under Article 21 on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will stop affected processing unless compelling legitimate grounds or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims justify continuation. The app does not conduct direct marketing.

Contact kontakt@jonesderosso.com. We cannot remotely access data stored only on your device; you can change or delete it locally. You may also exercise your rights directly with external providers for data they process independently.

14. Right to complain

Under Article 77 GDPR you may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement.

The authority for private businesses in Bavaria is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany. Website: https://www.lda.bayern.de. You do not have to contact us first before filing a complaint.

15. Optional data and automated decisions

You have no statutory obligation to provide us with personal data to use this app. Necessary connection data arises when you fetch online content. Location, favourites and contacting us are optional. Without location permission only that map function is unavailable; without external requests, new events are unavailable.

The app does not use automated decision-making including profiling under Article 22 GDPR. Search, date and region filters simply organise content according to your selection and have no legal or similarly significant effect.

16. Security and changes

The app uses operating system protected app storage and HTTPS for configured online sources. Device, backup and external application security also depends on your settings. We do not promise additional end-to-end encryption of local favourites or third-party requests. External redirects and other providers’ privacy practices are outside our control.

We update this policy when functionality, processing or legal requirements change. The current version is available offline under Settings → Privacy policy. Updating this policy does not replace consent where new purposes require it.
