Data in the app

MountMoji requires no account and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking services. Stickers and the catalog are bundled with the app. Favorites and your language choice are saved in your device settings so they remain available after restarting. MountMoji does not send these settings to its own servers. Device backups depend on your operating system settings. Clearing the app data removes the local settings.

Purposes and legal bases

Local storage of your selections provides the app features you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Settings are saved at your request and are necessary for this functionality (Section 25(2)(2) TDDDG). Sharing occurs only following your explicit action. We process contact requests to respond (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual inquiries, otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; legitimate interest: responding to inquiries).

Sharing and WhatsApp

Only when you share a sticker or import a pack are the selected images and pack data passed to the chosen app or WhatsApp. On iOS, WhatsApp import temporarily uses the local clipboard with a 60-second expiration. Further processing is governed by the receiving app’s privacy policy. MountMoji does not receive information about your messages or contacts.

Retention and external services

Local settings remain until you remove individual favorites or clear the app data. On iOS, deleting the app removes them; offloading alone does not delete data. Email inquiries are deleted when fully resolved unless statutory retention obligations apply. The app embeds no advertising or analytics SDKs. App stores and receiving apps process data under their own responsibility and may process it outside the EU according to their terms. MountMoji does not send usage data to its own servers.

Contact and rights

If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide to handle your request. For privacy questions or requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, contact kontakt@jonesderosso.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
