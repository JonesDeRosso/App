MountMoji · Support

400 outdoor designs

MountMoji is available as a one-time app purchase, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Browse the gallery, save favorites and share transparent images. Ten WhatsApp packs contain 300 stickers; the iOS extension includes all 400 designs.

Support

Need help? Email kontakt@jonesderosso.com with your device, operating system version, MountMoji version and steps to reproduce the issue. Please exclude private messages and contacts.

Add WhatsApp packs

Install WhatsApp, open “Set Up” in MountMoji and tap “Add” next to a pack. Confirm the import in WhatsApp. Android can add remaining packs one after another. On iPhone, confirm each pack individually.

Share any design

Tap a design and choose “Send as image”. The receiving app decides how it handles transparent images. The gallery and search work without WhatsApp.

Apple Messages stickers

Open a conversation in Messages and find MountMoji among the apps or stickers. The sticker extension is installed with the iOS app.

Remove local settings

Remove individual favorites using the heart. Clear app data in Android system settings, or delete the app on iOS. Offloading on iOS does not remove the stored settings.
